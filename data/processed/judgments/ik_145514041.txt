This revision petition is filed under
section 46(1)
of the
Karnataka Rent Act, 1999, to set aside the impugned
judgment dated 12.07.2012 passed in HRC No.143/2010, on
the file of II Addl. Judge, Court of Small Causes at Bangalore
& etc.

This petition coming on for admission this day, the
court made the following:
2

ORDER
When the matter is taken up for hearing,

petitioner-landlord, learned counsel for petitioner and

learned counsel for respondent-tenant have filed a joint

memo, reading as hereunder:-
"JOINT MEMO

The Respondent/Tenant admits that
he has vacated the schedule premise and
delivered the Key of the vacant premises to
the Petitioner/Landlord in the open court on
28.9.2012.
The Respondent admits the refund of
Security deposit of Rs.21,350/- (Rupees
Twenty One Thousand Three hundred fifty
only) after deducting the arrears of Rent of
Rs.10,650/- (Rupees Ten Thousand Six
Hundred Fifty only) by the Petitioner by a/c
payee cheque dated 28.9.2012 bearing
No.198548.
3
The Petitioner/landlord admits the
receipt of the key of the schedule premises
from the Respondent/Tenant and submits
that has refunded the Security Deposit of
Rs.21,350/- (after giving deduction to the
arrears of rent of Rs.10,650/-) by A/c Payee
cheque bearing No.198548 dated: 28.9.2012
drawn on Bharat Co-operative Bank Ltd.,
Mathikere Branch, Bangalore-54.
The parties to the above petition
submit they have no other claim against
each other with respect to the schedule
premises.
Hence, the parties to the above Petition
pray that this Hon'ble Court be pleased to
dispose of the above Petition in terms of the
Joint Memo in the interest of justice and
equity."
2. The joint memo is accepted. In view of the joint

memo, the impugned order is modified. The petitioner-

landlord has refunded the security deposit of
4
Rs.21,350/-, in the form of cheque bearing No.198548

dated 28.09.2012 drawn in favour of respondent-tenant,

after deducting arrears of rent of Rs.10,650/-. The

respondent-tenant has given key of schedule premises

to petitioner-landlord.
3. The revision petition is disposed off in terms of the

joint memo filed by parties.
