                              JUDGMENT
1.     The present suit has been filed by the plaintiffs seeking a
decree of permanent injunction restraining the defendants, their
family members, associates, agents and other persons acting on their
behalf from forcibly dispossessing the plaintiffs from the tenanted
premises bearing property No. A-5/1, Industrial Area, Kirti Nagar,
New Delhi-110015, except by due process of law, and from
interfering with the plaintiffs' peaceful possession thereof.                               Digitally

2.     The case of the plaintiffs, as set out in the plaint, is that they
are joint tenants in respect of the suit premises comprising
approximately 20 square yards situated on the ground floor and first
floor of property bearing No. A-5/1, Kirti Nagar Industrial Area, New
Delhi, at a monthly rent of ₹500/-. It is pleaded that the plaintiffs have
been in possession of the premises for several decades and have been
regularly paying rent. It is further averred that although the defendant
No.1 accepted rent, he did not issue formal rent receipts and merely
acknowledged receipt on plain paper.
3.     The plaintiffs have further pleaded that the tenancy stood
admitted by defendant No.1 in earlier litigation. It is alleged that
disputes arose between the defendants regarding ownership of the
property, but the plaintiffs continued to remain lawful tenants.
According to the plaintiffs, defendant No.1, along with certain
associates, repeatedly threatened to dispossess them forcibly on
28.02.2022 and 11.03.2022 without recourse to law. It is also alleged
that the attorney of defendant No. 2 threatened the plaintiffs to pay
rent to defendant No.2 claiming ownership of the property.
4.     Summons were issued to the defendants. Despite service,
none appeared on behalf of the defendants. Vide order dated
11.09.2023, the defendants were proceeded ex parte.
5.     In order to prove their case, plaintiff No.1, Sh. Surjit Singh,
examined himself as PW-1 and tendered his affidavit Ex. PW-1/A
reiterating the averments made in the plaint. He relied upon the
following documents:                                                                Digitally

6.     The plaintiffs also examined PW-2, Sh. Piyush Kapoor,
Senior Manager, Indraprastha Gas Limited, who produced the Gas
Sale Agreement dated 04.04.2005 executed between Indraprastha
Gas Limited and Sachdeva Foundary Works in respect of the suit
premises. The same was exhibited as Ex. PW-2/A (Colly) (OSR).
7.     Since the defendants chose not to contest the proceedings, the
testimony of PW-1 and PW-2 has remained unchallenged and
unrebutted. There is nothing on record to disbelieve the evidence led
by the plaintiffs.
8.     Documents Ex. 2/A if it is read in its entity, shows the settled
possession of the suit property with the plaintiff no. 2. Further as per
the unrebutted testimony of plaintiff Surjeet Singh/PW1 also, it is
clear that the plaintiffs are in settled possession of the suit property.
9.     It is also settled law that disputes regarding ownership
between the defendants cannot authorize either of them to dispossess
the plaintiffs otherwise than in accordance with law and that a person
in lawful possession cannot be dispossessed except by following due
process of law. Even a landlord is not entitled to evict a tenant by use
of force or otherwise than through legal proceedings. The plaintiffs,
therefore, are entitled to protection of their possession by way of
permanent injunction.                                                                Digitally

10.      Accordingly, the suit deserves to be decreed.
Relief
         The suit is decreed in favour of the plaintiffs and against the
defendants.
A decree of permanent injunction is hereby passed restraining
the defendants, their family members, associates, agents, servants,
representatives or any other person acting on their behalf from
forcibly or unlawfully dispossessing the plaintiffs from the tenanted
premises bearing property No. A-5/1, Industrial Area, Kirti Nagar,
New Delhi-110015, more particularly shown in red colour in the site
plan Ex. PW-1/1, except by following due process of law.
The defendants are further restrained from causing any
unlawful interference in the peaceful possession and enjoyment of the
plaintiffs over the suit premises except in accordance with law.
The plaintiffs shall also be entitled to costs of the suit.
         Decree sheet be prepared accordingly.
